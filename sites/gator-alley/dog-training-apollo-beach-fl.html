Dog training in Apollo Beach

Apollo Beach: registration rules and a county dog park

Apollo Beach dog owners follow Hillsborough County's animal ordinances, and the county runs its own dog park in town. The rules on registration and rabies vaccination are the ones new owners most often miss.

Here are the specifics, each linked to the county page it came from.

Know the local rules

  • Hillsborough County requires all dogs, cats and ferrets four months or older to be registered (Sec. 6-22), renewed yearly or every three years depending on the rabies vaccine type.Source: Hillsborough County: commonly cited animal ordinances
  • The same county rules require a rabies vaccination for animals four months or older (Sec. 6-21). Fines for these violations start at $100.Source: Hillsborough County: commonly cited animal ordinances
Places to practice

Official places for Apollo Beach dogs to exercise.

  • Apollo Beach Dog Park. 664 Golf and Sea Blvd., Apollo Beach. The county asks you to take the leash off in the transition area before entering, bans food for dogs and people, and asks you to leave toys, including small splash pools, at home. Scoop bags are provided.Source: Hillsborough County: find a dog park

Heat and safety. Hillsborough County's extreme heat page also links to heat.gov's pet safety guidance. On a hot day, shorten the walk and carry water.Source: Hillsborough County: extreme heat information

What are the rules at the Apollo Beach Dog Park?

Hillsborough County's dog park rules ask owners to leave food and toys at home, fill any holes their dog digs, and keep anyone under 16 with an adult. Dogs declared dangerous or aggressive are not allowed, and the dog should stay leashed everywhere outside the fence.
