FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What to know before you call.

Where does training happen?

Dogs live and learn in Jacob’s real home environment in Parrish rather than a kennel facility, with exposure to household routines and real-world distractions.

Who trains my dog?

Jacob personally trains and cares for every dog throughout the program.

What happens when my dog comes home?

The program includes a detailed handover and ongoing support so you can continue the training consistently at home.

What ages and breeds are a fit?

Every dog is different. Share your dog’s age, breed, history and goals so Jacob can discuss the appropriate next step.

Can puppy owners start the conversation?

Yes, puppy owners can include their socialization, household-manners and foundation goals in the consultation request. Jacob can then discuss whether the current programs are appropriate.

Can you help with reactivity or aggression?

Behavior history matters. Be candid about fear, aggression, reactivity or bite history during the consultation so the situation can be discussed responsibly.

What does training cost?

Program fit and scope are discussed during the consultation. Contact Jacob for current availability and program details rather than relying on an assumed price.
