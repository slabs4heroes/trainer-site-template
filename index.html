Meet your trainer

Don Shaw,
owner of Ironside K9

With over 17 years of experience, Don works across basic obedience, advanced off-leash training, behavior modification, working dogs, personal protection and competitive canine sports.

As an experienced decoy and working dog specialist, he knows canine behavior, genetics, motivation and drive development inside out. His approach blends proven techniques, modern learning theory and hands-on experience to build confident, well-balanced, reliable dogs.

Don believes training belongs in everyday life, not just in a list of commands. He focuses on clear communication, building confidence and helping dogs succeed in the real world, including around distractions and in public. Family pets get the same dedication as high-level working dogs (doodles are his favorite).

Away from the field, Don spends his time with his wife, three daughters and their own pack of pups.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What does board & train mean?

Your dog stays and trains with Don while you get regular progress updates, then you come in for a go-home lesson where he hands the skills over to you. Ironside K9 offers 4, 6 and 8 week programs.

Which board & train program is right for my dog?

The 4-week program builds a strong obedience foundation and manners. The 6-week program adds reliable on-leash skills and off-leash foundations, as appropriate. The 8-week program builds reliable on- and off-leash obedience. Call Don and he will tell you honestly which one fits.

Will I see how my dog is doing?

Yes. The 4-week program includes daily updates and progress reports, the 6-week program regular photo and video updates, and the 8-week program daily photo and video updates plus a final video progress report.

What happens at the go-home lesson?

Don goes through every command and how to handle your dog, so you can keep the training going at home. The 4-week program also includes a customized homework plan for maintenance.

Do you offer follow-up lessons?

The 6 and 8 week programs include follow-up lessons for the life of the dog.

What kinds of dogs do you train?

Family pets and high-level working dogs alike. Don works in basic obedience, off-leash training, behavior modification, working dogs, personal protection and competitive canine sports. Doodles are his favorite.

Can you help with problem behaviors?

Yes. The programs address jumping, mouthiness, pulling, reactivity and anxiety, using structure, confidence building and impulse control.
