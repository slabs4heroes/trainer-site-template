What reactivity looks like

A reactive dog overreacts to a trigger: other dogs, people, bikes, skateboards or sounds. It can look like barking, lunging, growling or freezing, and it often comes from fear, frustration or over-excitement. Reactivity is not the same as aggression, but the two can overlap, which is why history matters.

Why an honest history matters

Before any plan is built, Jacob wants to know what has happened, where it happens and what safety considerations matter at home. Be candid about fear, aggression, reactivity or any bite history. That is how the situation can be discussed responsibly.

How the work is structured

  1. Understand the trigger. What sets the behavior off, how intense it is and how your dog recovers.
  2. Build calm and control. Structure, clear communication and daily routines help your dog build confidence.
  3. Practice in the real world. Training moves into public settings and everyday distractions.
  4. Handover and support. You receive a full walkthrough of the tools, commands and structure your dog needs at home.

What to expect

This is a personalized plan, not a guaranteed outcome, especially for aggression or serious reactivity cases. The goal is a dog who is calmer, safer and more manageable in real life.

Common questions

Questions owners ask.

Is a reactive dog the same as an aggressive dog?

Not always. Reactivity is an overreaction to triggers, while aggression involves intent to harm. They can overlap, so Jacob asks for a full history during the consultation.

Can you help a dog that has bitten someone?

Bite history needs honest context and careful planning. Share exactly what happened in the consultation so Jacob can discuss whether and how to proceed.

Where does reactive dog training happen?

In Jacob's real home environment in Parrish and in real-world settings, not a kennel facility or a group class.

Will my dog ever be fully cured?

Outcomes depend on the dog, the history and the work at home. Jacob provides a personalized plan and a detailed handover, not a guarantee.

Do you work with reactive dogs from Bradenton and Sarasota?

Yes. Gator Alley serves Parrish, Palmetto, Ellenton, Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch and Sarasota.

Which program covers reactivity?

Advanced Obedience, Off-Leash & Behavior Modification is designed for reactivity, anxiety and other difficult behavior.
