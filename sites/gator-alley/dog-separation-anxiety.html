Signs of separation anxiety

  • Barking, howling or whining when you leave or shortly after.
  • Destructive behavior near doors, windows or crates.
  • Accidents in the house despite being potty trained.
  • Drooling, panting or pacing, and following you from room to room.

Anxiety is not disobedience

A dog who panics when alone is distressed, not spiteful. Punishment usually makes anxiety worse. The work focuses on building calm, confidence and structure.

How Jacob approaches it

Jacob starts with your dog's history and routine, then builds structure, independence and calm through daily work in his home. The Advanced Obedience & Behavior Modification program is designed for anxiety and difficult behavior, with a detailed handover for continuing at home.

A note on severe cases

Severe separation anxiety can involve medical or veterinary support. Talk with your veterinarian if your dog is hurting itself or cannot eat or settle, and share what you know with Jacob.

Common questions

Questions owners ask.

How do I know if it is separation anxiety?

Look for distress that starts when you leave or prepare to leave, such as panic, destruction or constant vocalizing. A trainer or vet can help confirm.

Will a second dog fix it?

Not reliably. Anxiety is tied to being left, and another dog can sometimes make no difference.

Does crate training help?

For some dogs, yes, but a crate can worsen distress in others. Discuss your dog's response with Jacob.

Do you offer help beyond Parrish?

Yes. Gator Alley serves Palmetto, Ellenton, Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch and Sarasota.

Can this be fixed with virtual coaching?

Some owners benefit from virtual sessions. For serious concerns Jacob will discuss whether a virtual session is the right fit.

Which program covers separation anxiety?

Advanced Obedience, Off-Leash & Behavior Modification covers anxiety and other difficult behavior.
