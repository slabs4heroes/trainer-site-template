Gator Alley Dog Training

Training built around real life.

Designed for owners struggling with serious behavioral challenges—or those who simply want a highly advanced level of obedience. Jacob works hands-on with reactive, aggressive, anxious, or difficult-to-manage dogs to address unwanted behaviors while developing exceptional obedience, calmness, confidence, and control.

Your dog will develop full-spectrum communication, advanced positioning, and off-leash reliability, creating a dog capable of performing reliably at home, in public, and around real-world distractions.

Program focus

  • Advanced obedience and real-world reliability
  • Off-leash communication and positioning
  • Reactivity, anxiety or difficult-to-manage behavior
  • Calmness and control around everyday distractions

How Advanced Obedience and Behavior Works

01

Personalized planning

Jacob discusses your dog’s specific behavior history — reactivity, anxiety or a bite history — before building a plan.

02

Focused, hands-on training

Jacob works directly with your dog every day to address the behavior while building advanced obedience and control.

03

Daily structure

Calm, consistent routines help your dog build confidence day after day, not just during a single session.

04

Real-world practice

Training moves into public settings and everyday distractions to build genuine off-leash reliability.

05

Owner handoff

You receive a full walkthrough of the tools, commands and structure your dog needs once home.

06

Consultation first

Every case starts with an honest conversation about your dog’s history — this is a personalized plan, not a guaranteed outcome, especially for aggression or reactivity cases.

Questions before you reach out.

A few things owners usually ask before their consultation.

Can you help with reactivity or aggression?

Behavior history matters. Be candid about fear, aggression, reactivity or a bite history during the consultation so the situation can be discussed responsibly — this is a personalized plan, not a guaranteed outcome, especially for aggression or reactivity cases.

Who trains my dog?

Jacob personally works with your dog throughout the program — no rotating staff or group classes.

What happens when my dog comes home?

The program includes a detailed handover and ongoing support so you can continue the training consistently and safely at home.
