Dog training in Venice

Venice: the only Sarasota County beach where dogs are allowed

Venice owners have an unusual local perk. The city's Brohard Paw Park leads to what the city calls the only beach area in Sarasota County where dogs are allowed.

That perk has rules, and so does the rest of the county. Here is what the official pages say.

Know the local rules

  • Brohard Paw Park is open daily from 7 a.m. to dusk, and dogs can use the beach only up to the posted sign that marks where the dog beach ends.Source: City of Venice: Brohard Paw Park
  • Sarasota County Sheriff's Animal Services requires a registration tag for dogs four months or older.Source: Sarasota County Sheriff: animal services registration
Places to practice

Official places for Venice dogs to exercise.

  • Brohard Paw Park. 1850 Harbor Drive S., Venice. A fenced dog play yard, plus access to the dog beach. The city notes the park is maintained by the county, and waste bags are provided.Source: City of Venice: Brohard Paw Park
  • Woodmere Park. Listed by Sarasota County Parks among its county-managed off-leash dog parks.Source: Sarasota County Parks: dog parks and dog-friendly parks

Heat and safety. Florida's emergency management agency lists heat rules for pets as well as people. At the beach, shade and fresh water matter as much as the swim.Source: Florida Division of Emergency Management: extreme heat
