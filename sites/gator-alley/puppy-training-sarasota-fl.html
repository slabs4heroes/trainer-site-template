Training built around your dog and your home.

The first months with a puppy set the habits you live with for years. Sarasota owners usually want the same things: a puppy who is potty trained, comfortable in a crate, calm around people and other dogs, and learning to walk on a leash before pulling becomes a habit.

Sarasota owners

Questions before you reach out.

Do you offer puppy training for Sarasota owners?

Yes. Gator Alley is based in Parrish and serves Sarasota owners with personally led board-and-train puppy programs, plus virtual coaching over Zoom. Contact Jacob to talk through logistics from Sarasota.

What is the difference between Puppy Head Start and Puppy Raise & Train?

Puppy Head Start is a focused 2-week program built around basic obedience, potty training and exposure training. Puppy Raise & Train is a longer 12-week program with structured routines, socialization and real-world exposure through a key stage of development.

How young can a puppy start?

Every puppy is different. Share your puppy's age, breed and your goals during the consultation so Jacob can discuss whether a program is appropriate and when to start.

Will my puppy be in a kennel?

No. Puppies live and learn in Jacob's real home environment in Parrish, with exposure to normal household routines rather than a kennel facility.

What does puppy training cost?

Program fit and scope are discussed during the free consultation. Contact Jacob for current availability and details rather than relying on an assumed price.
