New program

Puppy Head Start Program

2 weeks, designed specifically for puppies. This is a shorter, focused board-and-train built to give a young puppy the best possible start before habits — good or bad — become set.

What’s covered in 2 weeks

  • Basic obedience — sit, stay, place, name recognition and leash manners, introduced the right way for a young dog’s attention span.
  • Potty training — a consistent schedule and structure so the puppy goes home with reliable house-training habits already in place.
  • Exposure training — controlled introductions to new people, sounds, surfaces, and everyday situations so the puppy builds confidence instead of fear or overreaction as it grows.

Owners get a walkthrough at pickup on how to keep the routine going at home, so the two weeks of work keeps paying off long after the puppy leaves.

Questions about the programs

Before you choose a program.

Which program is right for my dog?

The right fit depends on your dog’s current behavior, training history and goals. Start with a consultation so Jacob can walk through the options together: the Custom Board & Train program, tailored around Basic Obedience, Behavioral Issues & Reactivity, or Puppy Foundations, with length of stay matched to the outcome you want — or, for puppies just starting out, the 2-week Puppy Head Start Program covering basic obedience, potty training and exposure training.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes — Jacob offers live virtual coaching over Zoom for owners who want one-on-one help with a specific topic, wherever they're located. The session is recorded and given to you afterward, plus follow-up support. See the Virtual Coaching Sessions section above for how it works.

Is virtual coaching a fit for every dog?

Not necessarily. For serious safety concerns or complex behavior histories, Jacob will first discuss the situation and confirm whether a virtual session is appropriate before booking.

Where will my dog stay?

Dogs live and learn in Jacob’s real home environment in Parrish rather than a kennel facility.

Who works with my dog?

Jacob personally trains and cares for every dog throughout the board-and-train program.

Do age and breed matter?

They are part of the fit conversation, along with training history, health, behavior and household goals. Include those details when you reach out.

What about reactivity, anxiety or aggression?

Behavior history should be discussed candidly, including fear, aggression, reactivity or bite history. Jacob can explain what information is needed before recommending a next step.

Do you use e-collars or balanced training methods?

Yes—Jacob’s approach combines voice, leash cues, hand signals and properly conditioned e-collar work as part of a balanced training method. This is discussed and agreed to as part of the program terms before training begins.

What should I expect on format and pricing?

These are immersive board-and-train programs in a real home environment, with handover and ongoing support. Current fit, availability and program details are discussed during a consultation.
