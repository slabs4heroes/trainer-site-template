Dog training in St. Petersburg

St. Petersburg: six city dog parks and a 6-foot leash rule

St. Petersburg is in Pinellas County, across Tampa Bay from Manatee County, so owners here often start with a live Zoom session. The city's public rules on leashes and dog parks are short and clear.

Both come from the city's own pages, linked below.

Know the local rules

  • The city's pet ownership guide says to keep dogs on a leash no longer than 6 feet in areas open to the public, including city parks (adapted from City Ordinance Sec. 4-55).Source: City of St. Petersburg: pet ownership guide
  • Dogs may be off leash only in fenced, designated dog parks.Source: City of St. Petersburg: pet ownership guide
Places to practice

Official places for St. Petersburg dogs to exercise.

  • Coquina Key. 3595 Locust St. SE.Source: St. Petersburg Parks & Recreation: dog parks
  • Crescent Lake. 1320 5th St. N.Source: St. Petersburg Parks & Recreation: dog parks
  • Kenwood Park. 401 20th St. N.Source: St. Petersburg Parks & Recreation: dog parks
  • Lake Vista Park. 1401 62nd Ave. S.Source: St. Petersburg Parks & Recreation: dog parks
  • North Shore. 901 North Shore Dr. NE.Source: St. Petersburg Parks & Recreation: dog parks
  • Walter Fuller. 7901 30th Ave. N. Parks are open from 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset and close briefly for weekly maintenance.Source: St. Petersburg Parks & Recreation: dog parks

Heat and safety. Florida's emergency management agency says a parked car can reach 135 degrees in under ten minutes. Keep dogs out of the car on bay-side errands.Source: Florida Division of Emergency Management: extreme heat

Dog training in St. Petersburg

Questions St. Petersburg owners ask.

Do you offer dog training in St. Petersburg, FL?

Yes. Gator Alley Dog Training is based in Parrish and serves St. Petersburg owners with personally led board-and-train programs, plus live virtual coaching over Zoom. Contact Jacob to talk through logistics from St. Petersburg.

Where can a St. Petersburg dog run off leash?

Only in the city's fenced dog parks: Coquina Key, Crescent Lake, Kenwood, Lake Vista, North Shore and Walter Fuller. Everywhere else in public the leash can be no longer than six feet.
