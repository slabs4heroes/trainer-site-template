What we offer

Four board-and-train programs, plus virtual coaching.

Choose the track that fits your dog’s age, behavior history and goals.

Track 01 · 2 weeks

Puppy Head Start Program

A focused 2-week program for puppies covering basic obedience, potty training and exposure training — the best possible start before habits set in.

Explore Puppy Head Start
Track 02 · Board & Train

Basic Obedience

A focused program for clearer communication, better manners, reliable routines and practical obedience matched to your household.

Explore Basic Obedience
Track 03 · Advanced

Advanced Obedience, Off-Leash & Behavior Modification

For owners navigating reactivity, anxiety, difficult behavior, or a higher standard of advanced obedience, calmness, communication and off-leash reliability.

Explore Advanced Obedience
Track 04 · 12 weeks

Puppy Raise & Train

A longer foundation-focused program personally led by Jacob during the key early stages of development, with structured routines, socialization, real-world exposure and advanced foundations.

Explore Puppy Raise & Train
