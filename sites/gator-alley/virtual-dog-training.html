Gator Alley Dog Training

Training built around your dog and your home.

Virtual coaching is for dog owners who want live, one-on-one help with a specific topic. Jacob works through what you are dealing with over Zoom, with instruction tailored to your dog and your home, and the session is recorded so you can rewatch it.

Afterward you get instructional videos connected to what you covered, plus follow-up support to ask questions and check on progress. For serious safety concerns such as a bite history or significant aggression, Jacob will first discuss whether a virtual session is the right fit.

Dog owners anywhere

Questions before you reach out.

What is virtual dog training?

Live one-on-one coaching with Jacob over Zoom. You bring a specific topic, such as leash manners, puppy foundations or behavior and consistency, and work through it with instruction tailored to your dog and your home.

Are the sessions recorded?

Yes. Each session is recorded so you can rewatch it, and you get instructional videos connected to what you covered.

Who can use virtual coaching?

Owners in Parrish, Bradenton, Sarasota, Tampa and anywhere else. All you need is a Zoom connection and your dog.

Can virtual coaching help with aggression or a bite history?

Serious safety concerns need a careful conversation first. Jacob will discuss whether a virtual session is the right fit before booking.

How much do sessions cost and how long are they?

Jacob confirms session details, availability and pricing when you reach out. Request a consultation to ask.
