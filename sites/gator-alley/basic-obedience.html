How Basic Obedience Works

01

Personalized planning

Jacob talks through the specific behaviors and goals you want to address before training starts, so the program fits your dog and your household.

02

Focused daily training

Jacob works one-on-one with your dog inside his own home, building manners, clearer communication and everyday obedience day by day.

03

Real-world practice

Skills are practiced around the distractions of daily life — walks, greetings, guests — not just inside a quiet training space.

04

Owner handoff

Before your dog comes home, you get a full walkthrough of the commands and routines so the training holds up at home.

05

Consultation first

Every program starts with a conversation about your dog’s history and goals — not a one-size-fits-all sign-up.

Questions before you reach out.

A few things owners usually ask before their consultation.

Where does training happen?

Training happens in Jacob’s real home environment in Parrish rather than a kennel facility, with exposure to normal household routines and real-world distractions.

Who trains my dog?

Jacob personally trains and cares for your dog throughout the program — no rotating staff.

What ages and breeds are a fit for Basic Obedience?

Every dog is different. Share your dog’s age, breed, history and goals during the consultation so Jacob can discuss whether this program is the right fit.
