Local dog training

Dog training for Venice owners

Venice is a Gulf Coast town of walkable downtown streets, beaches, the Legacy Trail and one of the area's best-known dog parks. Dogs here meet crowds, bikes, water and other dogs, which makes calm leash skills and a reliable recall far more important than a perfect sit in the living room.

Gator Alley is based in Parrish and welcomes board-and-train clients from around the region. Jacob will talk through logistics from Venice, and live virtual coaching over Zoom is available for specific topics like leash manners and puppy foundations.

Owners here walk and train around Brohard Paw Park, the Legacy Trail, downtown Venice and the beaches along the Gulf. Venice owners often start with Basic Obedience, Puppy Head Start or one-on-one virtual coaching.

What Venice owners ask for

  • Downtown and trail walks. Loose-leash walking around crowds, bikes and other dogs.
  • Beach and dog-park outings. Recall, impulse control and polite play with other dogs.
  • Puppy foundations. Potty and crate routines and early obedience for new puppy owners.

Dog training in Venice

Questions Venice owners ask.

Do you offer dog training in Venice, FL?

Yes. Gator Alley Dog Training is based in Parrish and serves Venice owners with personally led board-and-train programs, plus live virtual coaching over Zoom. Contact Jacob to talk through logistics from Venice.

Which programs are available to Venice owners?

Four board-and-train programs: Puppy Head Start (2 weeks), Basic Obedience, Advanced Obedience, Off-Leash & Behavior Modification, and Puppy Raise & Train (12 weeks). Jacob also offers live one-on-one virtual coaching sessions over Zoom.

Will my dog be kenneled?

No. Dogs live and learn in Jacob’s real home environment in Parrish rather than a kennel facility, and Jacob personally trains and cares for every dog throughout the program.

Can you help with reactivity, anxiety or aggression?

Behavior history matters. Be candid about fear, aggression, reactivity or any bite history during the consultation so the situation can be discussed responsibly. It is a personalized plan, not a guaranteed outcome.

What does training cost?

Program fit and scope are discussed during the free consultation. Contact Jacob for current availability and program details rather than relying on an assumed price.
