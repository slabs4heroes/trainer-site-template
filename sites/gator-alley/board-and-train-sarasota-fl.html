Gator Alley Dog Training

Training built around your dog and your home.

Board and train gives your dog focused, daily work with one trainer, followed by a handover so you can keep the results going at home. Sarasota owners usually come to us after months of leash pulling, jumping, barking or recall that works only when there are no distractions.

At Gator Alley, Jacob personally trains and cares for every dog throughout the program in his own home in Parrish. There are no rotating staff and no group classes. The length of stay is set after the consultation, based on what your dog needs and the outcome you want.

Sarasota owners

Questions before you reach out.

Do you offer board and train for Sarasota dogs?

Yes. Gator Alley is based in Parrish and welcomes board-and-train clients from Sarasota and across the area. Contact Jacob to discuss logistics and which program fits.

How long does board and train take?

The length of stay is not fixed in advance. It is set after the consultation, based on your dog's needs and the outcome you want.

Can you help with reactivity or aggression?

Behavior history matters. Be candid about fear, aggression, reactivity or any bite history during the consultation so the situation can be discussed responsibly. It is a personalized plan, not a guaranteed outcome.

Is my dog kenneled?

No. Dogs live and learn in Jacob's real home environment rather than a kennel facility.

What if I cannot make it to Parrish?

Jacob also offers live one-on-one virtual coaching over Zoom for topics like leash manners, puppy foundations and consistency.
