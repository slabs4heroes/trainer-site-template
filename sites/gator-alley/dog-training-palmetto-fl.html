Dog training in Palmetto

Palmetto: a county dog park and a preserve on 17th Street West

Jacob was born and raised in Palmetto. Palmetto's official dog options sit with Manatee County, which publishes both its dog park rules and its preserve policies online.

Here is what the county says, with a link to each page.

Know the local rules

  • Manatee County publishes two numbers on its dog park page: Animal Services at (941) 747-5933 and the Sheriff's Office at (941) 747-3011.Source: Manatee County: dog parks
  • All of the county's preserves have marked trails, and the county asks people and dogs to stay on them because off-trail use damages habitat.Source: Manatee County: parks and preserves pet policy
  • Dogs are allowed in county preserves on a leash, except Leffis Key, Moody Branch, Neal and Perico.Source: Manatee County 311: dogs in county preserves
Places to practice

Official places for Palmetto dogs to exercise.

  • Blackstone Park dog park. 2112 14th Avenue West, Palmetto. The county lists large-dog and small-dog areas.Source: Manatee County: dog parks
  • Emerson Point Preserve. 5801 17th Street West, Palmetto. The county lists it as open daily from sunrise to sunset. The preserve lists a beach, and the county does not allow dogs at county beaches, so follow posted signs.Source: Manatee County: natural resources and preserves; Manatee County: parks and preserves pet policy

Heat and safety. The National Weather Service Tampa Bay office posts heat tools for West Central Florida. Check the heat index before a midday walk.Source: National Weather Service Tampa Bay: heat tools

Dog training in Palmetto

Questions Palmetto owners ask.

Do you offer dog training in Palmetto, FL?

Yes. Gator Alley Dog Training is based in Parrish and serves Palmetto owners with personally led board-and-train programs, plus live virtual coaching over Zoom. Contact Jacob to talk through logistics from Palmetto.

Where can my Palmetto dog run off leash?

Blackstone Park's dog park is the county's listed option in Palmetto. Inside the fence dogs are off leash, and they must be leashed entering and leaving. The county asks for a current license tag and rabies vaccination.
