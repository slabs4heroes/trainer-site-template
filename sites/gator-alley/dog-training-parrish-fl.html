Dog training in Parrish

Parrish dogs live under Manatee County rules

Gator Alley Dog Training is at 10328 Charlotte Dr in Parrish, so for Parrish owners the training home is right in town. What a trainer can build in a few weeks still has to work inside the rules your dog lives under every day, and in Parrish those are Manatee County's.

Below are the county and state rules that matter most when you walk, license and exercise a dog in the Parrish area, each tied to the official page it came from.

Know the local rules

  • Manatee County asks owners of dogs, cats and ferrets to keep a yearly rabies vaccination and a county license, and it will not sell a license without proof of a current rabies shot.Source: Manatee County: license a pet
  • The county lists fines of $200, $350 and $500 for a first, second and third failure to get a license tag, and says non-registration can reach $1,000.Source: Manatee County: license a pet
  • In county parks and preserves a dog has to be on a leash the whole time.Source: Manatee County: parks and preserves pet policy
Places to practice

Official places for Parrish dogs to exercise.

  • Ola Mae Sims Park Happy Paws Dog Park. 11800 Erie Road, Parrish. The county lists separate large-dog and small-dog areas.Source: Manatee County: dog parks
  • Rye Preserve. 905 Rye Wilderness Trail. County preserves welcome leashed dogs, with four exceptions (Leffis Key, Moody Branch, Neal and Perico).Source: Manatee County 311: dogs in county preserves
  • Lake Manatee State Park. On State Road 64 east of I-75. Pets are allowed on a 6-foot hand-held leash and may not be left unattended.Source: Florida State Parks: Lake Manatee State Park; Florida State Parks: Lake Manatee brochure

Heat and safety. Manatee County's pet policy page carries a “Keep it Cool” reminder for hot days, and notes that not every trail has shade. Pack water for your dog before a summer walk.Source: Manatee County: parks and preserves pet policy

Where is Gator Alley Dog Training located?

What does my Parrish dog legally need before it goes to a dog park?

The county's dog park rules ask for a current license tag and a current rabies vaccination, a leash on the way in and out, and an owner who stays in view with a leash in hand. Dogs in heat and aggressive dogs are not allowed to stay.
