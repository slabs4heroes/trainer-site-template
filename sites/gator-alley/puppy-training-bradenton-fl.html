Gator Alley Dog Training

Training built around your dog and your home.

A new puppy in Bradenton means new routines: potty breaks before work, crate time, walks that do not turn into tug-of-war, and a puppy who has to learn manners around visitors, kids and other dogs.

Gator Alley Dog Training is a short trip from Bradenton. Jacob trains puppies in his own home in Parrish, building the routines, early obedience and real-world exposure that make life at home easier, then walks you through a handover so you can continue the work.

Why owners choose Gator Alley

  • Personally led by Jacob, no rotating staff
  • A real home environment, not a kennel
  • Real-world practice around everyday distractions
  • A detailed handover and ongoing support
  • Consultation first, with no one-size-fits-all promise

Bradenton owners

Questions before you reach out.

Do you train puppies for Bradenton owners?

Yes. Gator Alley serves Bradenton owners from Parrish with personally led puppy board-and-train programs. Contact Jacob to talk through the logistics.

Where does my puppy stay during training?

In Jacob's real home environment in Parrish, not a kennel facility, so your puppy practices around normal household routines.

What happens when my puppy comes home?

Each program includes a detailed handover and ongoing support so you can keep the routines, commands and expectations going consistently.

Can I start with a conversation before choosing a program?

Yes. Share your puppy's age, breed and your goals in the consultation request, and Jacob can discuss whether Puppy Head Start or Puppy Raise & Train is the right fit.

What does it cost?

Program fit and scope are discussed during the free consultation. Contact Jacob for current availability and program details.
